Apex GP

Online race

Race your friends. One person creates a room and shares the 5-letter code; everyone else joins with it. The host's circuit, race distance and weather are used. Pick your team and driver at the bottom first.

    Rename teams and drivers

    Give any team or driver the name you want, plus the three-letter code shown in the timing tower and the race number. Names are saved on this device and used everywhere: menus, timing, results and career.

    Settings

    The physics are the same with or without assists: a tyre model with temperature and wear, aero load that builds with speed, weight transfer, and a power unit that deploys and harvests electric energy. Assists help you stay inside the limits.

    Straight-mode zones are marked green on the minimap. In a race you get overtake mode for a lap when you cross the line within one second of the car ahead. Hold it to deploy full electric power at high speed.

    ActionKeyboardController / wheel

    Gamepads work straight away. For a wheel and pedals, bind steer left, steer right, throttle and brake: press Bind, then turn or press that control fully.

    Browsers cannot drive wheel force feedback, so wheels run without it. Gamepads get rumble on kerbs, lock-ups and impacts.

    Touch controls (phone and tablet)

    On a phone, turn it sideways. With the F1 wheel, put your thumb on the wheel and turn it like the real thing. Slide up a pedal for more throttle or brake. Paddle-shift buttons appear when the gearbox is set to manual. Tilt steering isn't available, because the game page can't read the phone's motion sensors.

    With your own music, start a playlist in Spotify first, then open the game: its engine and effects play over your music instead of stopping it.

    During a race: N next track, K play/pause. The music needs an internet connection; offline the built-in hardstyle plays instead.

    Nothing playing

    During a race: N next track, K play/pause. Spotify plays on your phone, computer or speaker; on a computer with Spotify Premium the game itself can be the player.
